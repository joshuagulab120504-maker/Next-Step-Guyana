# Guyana Career Pathway Guide
*Verified working edition: school subjects, training routes, professional gates and first jobs*

**Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026  
**Status:** Source-led working reference for a career/gap-analysis system. It is not a guarantee of admission, funding, licensing or employment.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Each career entry separates four things that are often incorrectly combined:

1. **Helpful school subjects** — subjects that prepare a student for the field. These are recommendations, not automatic national requirements.
2. **Current route** — the degree, diploma, apprenticeship, examination or portfolio route confirmed from a current official or first-party source.
3. **Professional gate** — the internship, council registration, licence, examination, appointment or competitive selection required after education.
4. **First-job route** — where vacancies or early-career opportunities normally arise. An employer named here is an example, not a hiring promise.

Status marks apply only to the existence and general shape of the route:

- ✅ **Current route confirmed:** a current official or first-party page supports the route. Exact subjects, fees and intake dates must still be checked for the intended year.
- 🟡 **Variable or incomplete route:** the occupation exists, but Guyana has no single standard pathway or a current detail remains unpublished.
- ⚠️ **Portfolio, talent or appointment route:** education helps, but credentials alone do not control entry.

Do not encode a whole career entry under one confidence flag. Store `source_url`, `source_title`, `retrieved_at`, `effective_intake`, `campus_or_region`, `credential`, `regulator`, `last_verified` and `claim_status` for every material rule.

For student-facing pages, show three checkpoints where they apply: **Forms 1–3** for exploration and foundations; **Forms 4–5** for CSEC/CVQ subject and portfolio choices; and **Form 6 or post-CSEC** for CAPE, tertiary study, apprenticeship, licensing or first employment. A student should never be told that one early choice permanently closes every alternative route.

## NATIONAL EDUCATION, TRAINING AND EMPLOYMENT LAYERS

### University of Guyana tuition

For Guyanese students, UG states that tuition is free for new and continuing Diploma, Bachelor's, Master's and PhD students from 1 January 2025. Its current registration procedure says approved Guyanese students attend under the Government of Guyana free-tuition arrangement; the current fee page is headed for non-Guyanese students. Free tuition does not guarantee a place in a competitive programme, and applicants should still check application deadlines and non-tuition obligations. Sources: [UG free-tuition notice](https://uog.edu.gy/free-tuition-takes-effect-new-academic-year-202526-ug-applications-will-close-may-16-2025-students), [UG registration procedure](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/admissions-division/procedure-registration), [UG tuition and fees](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/admissions-division/tuition-fees).

### Ministry of Labour and Manpower Planning

- The [National Job Bank](https://labour.gov.gy/jobs-bank/) lists vacancies and connects job seekers with employers.
- The **Board of Industrial Training (BIT)** provides free vocational training nationally. Course availability, duration, stipend and final credential vary by programme and intake; see Category K and the Ministry's [BIT updates](https://labour.gov.gy/category/board-of-industrial-training-bit/).
- The first National Youth Skills Forum and Skills Search Expo was held in July 2026. It should be described as an **inaugural event**, not yet as a recurring annual programme. Source: [DPI Youth Skills Forum report](https://dpi.gov.gy/youth-skills-forum-promotes-workforce-readiness/).
- Labour-force statistics are published principally through the **Bureau of Statistics' Guyana Labour Force Survey**. Do not attribute all labour-force and wage surveys to a Ministry LMIS unit without a specific source. See [Bureau of Statistics publications](https://statisticsguyana.gov.gy/publications/).

### Region and access

Region is as important as grades. Georgetown still concentrates many degree and professional routes, but regional access is expanding:

- UG's MBBS process includes regional considerations for applicants from underserved regions. Source: [UG MBBS admission criteria](https://cms.uog.edu.gy/school-medicine/bachelor-medicine-bachelor-surgery-mbbs-0/admission-criteria).
- From 2026, UG's College of Medical Sciences began a regional pre-medical foundation route intended to reach all ten regions. Source: [UG regional medical-sciences initiative](https://uog.edu.gy/press-release-university-guyana-college-medical-sciences-delivering-foundation-modern-medical).
- The Ministry of Education reported in 2026 that 94 secondary schools across all ten regions were offering CVQ programmes. Source: [94 schools offering CVQ](https://education.gov.gy/en/index.php/media2/external-news/10822-94-schools-now-offering-cvq-programmes).
- New or upgraded practical-instruction facilities include Bartica, Beterverwagting and St Ignatius. The system should therefore store the actual institution and region instead of assuming every TVET path requires Georgetown relocation.

### Hinterland Scholarship Programme ✅

The Ministry of Amerindian Affairs' programme dates to 1962 and supports secondary and technical/vocational study. Published support includes accommodation, transportation and education-related allowances; older students without local secondary access can use vocational routes, subject to the current application criteria. University-level scholarship responsibility moved to the national public-service scholarship system in 2000. Sources: [Hinterland Scholarships](https://moaa.gov.gy/hinterland-scholarships/), [Hinterland Scholarship Division](https://moaa.gov.gy/hinterland-scholarship-division/).

Do **not** encode the old figures of 295 students, 700+ secondary students or an 83% CSEC pass rate as current programme facts. They are historical figures without a current cohort/year in the earlier draft. Likewise, the historical “13% to 45%” claim arose in an NGSA/primary-education context and must not be represented as a general or CSEC hinterland pass-rate trend.

# CATEGORY A: BUSINESS, FINANCE & ENTREPRENEURSHIP

## Banker / Banking Operations ✅

**Helpful subjects:** English, Mathematics, Principles of Accounts, Principles of Business and Economics.  
**Current routes:** UG SEBI programmes in Accountancy, Finance or Management; Diploma in Banking and Finance; or Republic Bank's Youth Link apprenticeship when an intake is advertised. SEBI degree entry generally uses five CSEC subjects including English and Mathematics. Youth Link currently leads to a CVQ Level 1 in Banking Operations, but the live page does not support hard-coding the earlier “ages 16–19” and “principal-nominated” rules for every intake. Sources: [UG SEBI programmes](https://sebi.uog.edu.gy/sebi-glance), [Republic Bank Youth Link](https://www.republicguyana.com/node/1277).  
**First jobs:** bank teller, customer-service representative, credit assistant, operations clerk, analyst trainee and apprenticeship placements.

## Accountant / Auditor ✅

**Helpful subjects:** English, Mathematics, Principles of Accounts, Economics and business subjects.  
**Current routes:** BSc Accountancy or related SEBI route, followed where desired by ACCA or another recognised professional qualification. The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Guyana admits qualified members of recognised overseas accountancy bodies; Guyana does not operate a separate local chartered-accountant examination. Sources: [UG BSc Accountancy](https://sebi.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/246/programmes/865/details), [ICAG membership](https://icag.org.gy/membership/membership-application/).  
**Current development:** UG's accountancy programme received ACCA accreditation for 2026–2030, with four exemptions reported for qualifying graduates from 2026. Confirm the exemption calculator against the student's graduation year. Source: [UG/ACCA accreditation report](https://newsroom.gy/2026/06/23/ug-now-accredited-to-offer-acca-certification/).  
**First jobs:** accounts assistant, audit associate, tax assistant, finance officer and internal-audit trainee.

## Entrepreneur / Business Owner ✅

A degree is optional. Routes include UG's BSc Entrepreneurship, business or technical training, and direct entry into a trade or service. Formal milestones include registering the business, meeting tax and sector-licensing rules, opening business banking facilities and arranging finance. The [Small Business Bureau](https://sbb.gov.gy/) publishes grant, loan and development-support information. Business registration is not evidence that a venture is funded or commercially viable.

## Marketing / Advertising Professional ✅

**Helpful subjects:** English, business subjects, visual arts and information technology.  
**Route:** UG's BSc Marketing, Communication Studies, portfolio-based digital work or recognised short courses. Source: [UG BSc Marketing](https://www.sebi.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/248/programmes/869/details).  
**First jobs:** marketing assistant, sales-development representative, communications assistant, social-media coordinator and junior designer.

## Human Resources Professional 🟡

Common routes include Management, Psychology, Labour Studies or Business qualifications, followed by role-specific experience or international HR certifications. There is no single Guyanese licence for general HR practice. Entry rules belong to the vacancy, not to the occupation as a whole.

## Economics and Supply Chain 🟡

Relevant routes include Economics, Finance, Accountancy, Management and UG's BSc Supply Chain Management. Careers include economist, statistician, procurement officer, purchasing assistant and logistics coordinator. Professional or employer-specific certifications may apply; check the current UG [offered-programmes list](https://www.turkeyenonline.uog.edu.gy/srms/student/prospective/about_applying/offeredprogs.php) and individual vacancies.

## Insurance / AML / Compliance Professional 🟡

**Forms 1–3:** build numeracy, written communication, digital literacy and careful record-keeping.  
**Forms 4–5:** Mathematics, English, Principles of Accounts, Principles of Business and Economics are useful; work on spreadsheet and report-writing skills.  
**Form 6 or post-CSEC:** routes include Accountancy, Finance, Economics, Law, Management or entry-level insurer/bank training, followed by role-specific insurance, risk, anti-money-laundering or compliance education. Guyana has no single school-leaver licence covering this whole group, and vacancy requirements differ.  
**First jobs:** underwriting assistant, claims assistant, compliance assistant, KYC/onboarding officer and risk or audit trainee.

## Office Administration / Customer Service / Sales ✅

**Forms 1–3:** practise English, numeracy, keyboarding, email etiquette, teamwork and public speaking.  
**Forms 4–5:** English, Mathematics, EDPM/Information Technology and business subjects are useful. School CVQ occupational areas have included customer service, business administration and data administration. Source: [Ministry of Education school-TVET pathways](https://education.gov.gy/en/index.php/media2/external-news/5053-job-ready-technical-skills-for-all-secondary-students-in-the-future).  
**Form 6 or post-CSEC:** students may enter work after CSEC/CVQ, pursue office-administration or business training, or continue to a diploma/degree. Use the vacancy—not a universal “five subjects” rule—for the actual minimum.  
**First jobs:** receptionist, administrative assistant, records/data clerk, customer-service representative, retail associate and sales assistant. The [National Job Bank](https://labour.gov.gy/jobs-bank/) is a live source for requirements.

# CATEGORY B: LAW, JUSTICE & PUBLIC SERVICE

## Lawyer / Attorney-at-Law ✅

**Helpful subjects:** English, History, Communication Studies and other writing-intensive subjects.  
**Current route:** UG LLB through the published CAPE/GCE route or the Preliminary Law route. The current Preliminary route includes a minimum GPA requirement; use the live programme page rather than a generic subject template. Source: [UG LLB admission details](https://fss.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/62/programmes/571/details).  
**Professional gate:** the current regional arrangement gives 25 Guyanese UG graduates annual direct access to the Hugh Wooding Law School two-year Legal Education Certificate; other routes may require the Council of Legal Education entrance process. The list is submitted by UG—not accurately described as a “gazetted list.” Sources: [Hugh Wooding two-year programme](https://hwls.edu.tt/two-year-programme/), [Hugh Wooding equal-opportunity policy](https://hwls.edu.tt/equal-opportunity-policy/).  
**Current development:** a regional law school in Guyana was at the design-tender stage in July 2026. It is not yet an operating substitute for Hugh Wooding, but the government says it is intended to remove the present cap. Source: [DPI law-school update](https://dpi.gov.gy/guyanas-law-school-will-eliminate-guyanese-student-admission-limits/).

## Magistrate / Judge 🟡

These are appointed legal offices, not school-leaver occupations. The route begins with qualification and admission as an attorney, followed by substantial relevant practice and appointment through the applicable constitutional or judicial process. Serving as a magistrate is **not** a universal prerequisite to becoming a judge; remove that progression as a hard rule.

## Diplomat / Foreign Service Officer ✅

UG's BSocSc International Relations is a relevant route and its current entry requirements do not make Mathematics compulsory. Languages, History, Economics, Political Science and Communication Studies are useful. Recruitment occurs when the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation advertises roles; no permanent school-to-diplomat guarantee exists. Source: [UG International Relations](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/66/programmes/96/details).

## Civil / Public Servant 🟡

There is no universal “five CSEC subjects” rule for every government job. Clerical, technical, professional, administrative and senior roles carry different vacancy-specific requirements. Use official ministry, agency and National Job Bank advertisements as the controlling source. Scholarships may include service obligations, but a bond must be verified from the actual award terms.

## Customs Officer / GRA Officer 🟡

Customs is a Guyana Revenue Authority function. Requirements vary by advertised role and grade; do not encode a standing CSEC minimum for all GRA jobs. For example, some boarding or petroleum-unit roles require technical or tertiary qualifications and experience. Source: [GRA careers](https://gra.gov.gy/careers/).

## Immigration Officer 🟡

Immigration is **not** a GRA occupation. It falls under the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Guyana Police Force's immigration function. Recruitment rules depend on the relevant Police/Home Affairs vacancy. Source: [Ministry of Home Affairs agencies](https://moha.gov.gy/list-of-agencies/).

# CATEGORY C: HEALTH SCIENCES

## Medical Doctor ✅

**Helpful subjects:** English, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry and Physics.  
**Current route:** UG MBBS through one of its published admission categories, including recognised CAPE/GCE science routes and approved tertiary-science routes. Do not reduce the nine-category process to one CSEC sentence. The programme is competitive and includes regional considerations. Source: [UG MBBS admission criteria](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/259/programmes/1169/details).  
**Professional gate:** registration with the Medical Council of Guyana, supervised internship and the appropriate institutional/full-registration steps. Source: [Medical Council registration requirements](https://medicalcouncil.org.gy/index.php/requirements-for-registration).  
**First jobs:** supervised internship and public/private hospital appointments; employment, bond and placement terms depend on the intake and sponsor. Do not hard-code a single licensing age.

## Specialist Doctor ✅

Specialist medicine is no longer merely a study-abroad gap. UG's current offerings include postgraduate training in general surgery, orthopaedics, psychiatry, anaesthesia and intensive care, family medicine, internal medicine, emergency medicine, obstetrics and gynaecology, paediatrics, cardiology, diagnostic radiology, neurosurgery, urology and otolaryngology, among others. Entry normally requires a medical degree, registration and clinical experience. Source: [UG offered programmes](https://www.turkeyenonline.uog.edu.gy/srms/student/prospective/about_applying/offeredprogs.php).

## Dentist ✅

UG currently lists the **Bachelor of Dental Surgery**. Applicants should use the live BDS requirements and offered-programme status for the intended intake rather than assuming its requirements are identical to MBBS. Source: [UG offered programmes](https://www.turkeyenonline.uog.edu.gy/srms/student/prospective/about_applying/offeredprogs.php). Professional practice requires registration with the Guyana Dental Council, which registers dental practitioners, dentist extenders and dental technicians. Source: [Guyana Dental Council](https://health.gov.gy/guyana-dental-council/).

## Nurse / Midwife ✅

The Ministry of Health publishes training opportunities requiring five CSEC subjects including English, Mathematics and at least one science for its main professional-nursing route; exact age, health checks and subject combinations belong to the current notice. UG and approved hospital-based routes also exist. Nurses, midwives and nursing assistants must be registered with the **Nurses and Midwives Council, Guyana** before practising. Sources: [Ministry of Health training opportunities](https://health.gov.gy/training-opportunities/), [Nurses and Midwives Council](https://health.gov.gy/nursing-and-midwife-council/). Do not promise completion or registration by age 19–21.

## Medex / Community Health Worker ✅

**Forms 1–3:** strengthen English, Mathematics, Biology/Human and Social Biology and community-service skills.  
**Forms 4–5:** for the direct Medex alternative pathway, preserve English, Mathematics and at least one accepted science. The current Ministry page publishes five CSEC subjects for this route.  
**Post-CSEC routes:** the Ministry lists a 42-month direct Medex route with a five-year contractual obligation, plus an 18-month post-basic route for experienced registered nurses/midwives. It also lists a six-month Community Health Worker route requiring two CSEC subjects including English, with a one-year obligation. These terms are intake-specific. Source: [Ministry of Health training opportunities](https://health.gov.gy/training-opportunities/).  
**First jobs:** community health work and supervised public-health assignments. The Ministry describes progression from Community Health Worker toward Nursing Assistant, Registered Nurse, Midwifery and Medex. Source: [2025 regional CHW pathway](https://health.gov.gy/press_release/third-batch-of-community-health-workers-graduates-in-region-seven-through-the-health-sciences-division-decentralised-programme/).

## Emergency Medical Technician / Emergency Services Technician 🟡

**Forms 1–3:** develop science foundations, physical fitness, calm communication and first-aid awareness.  
**Forms 4–5:** Biology/Human and Social Biology, Integrated Science, English, Mathematics and Physical Education are helpful.  
**Post-CSEC:** enter only through a currently approved emergency-care training route; the earlier research did not confirm a single permanently open school-leaver intake. EMT and Emergency Services Technician are regulated categories under the Allied Health Professions Council, so recognised training and registration are required. Source: [AHPC professions](https://www.alliedhealthguyana.com/about).  
**First jobs:** ambulance/emergency-service roles after training, registration and employer selection.

## Physiotherapist / Rehabilitation Professional ✅

**Forms 1–3:** build Biology, science, communication, empathy and physical-activity foundations.  
**Forms 4–5:** UG's direct BSc Medical Rehabilitation (Physiotherapy) route lists five CSEC subjects including English, Mathematics and at least one accepted science.  
**Alternative route:** a Rehabilitation Assistant Certificate plus the published grades, experience and performance requirements can also lead into the degree. Source: [UG BSc Medical Rehabilitation—Physiotherapy](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/259/programmes/1154/details).  
**Professional gate:** Physiotherapist, Physical Therapist, Occupational Therapist, Rehabilitation Assistant and Speech-Language/Audiology categories are regulated by the Allied Health Professions Council. Source: [AHPC professions](https://www.alliedhealthguyana.com/about).

## Radiographer / Medical-Imaging or Sonography Professional ✅

**Forms 1–3:** focus on Mathematics, Physics, Biology and careful technical work.  
**Forms 4–5:** the Ministry's X-ray Technician route currently lists four CSEC subjects including English, Mathematics and an accepted science; UG also offers BSc Medical Imaging. Sources: [Ministry of Health training opportunities](https://health.gov.gy/training-opportunities/), [UG health-sciences programmes](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/health-sciences).  
**Specialisation:** sonography may be an advanced route for existing health/imaging personnel rather than a permanent direct school-leaver programme; a one-year hybrid Ministry/Mohawk programme was launched for a selected cohort. Source: [Ministry sonography programme](https://health.gov.gy/press_release/moh-mohawk-college-launch-advanced-level-sonography-training-programme/).  
**Professional gate:** Radiographer, Medical Imaging Technologist, Sonographer, Ultrasound Sonographer and X-ray Technician are AHPC categories.

## Dentex / Dental Assistant / Dental Technician ✅

**Forms 1–3:** strengthen English, science, fine-motor skills and patient communication.  
**Forms 4–5:** the Ministry currently lists a two-year Dentex route with four CSEC subjects including English, Mathematics and one accepted science, and a one-year Dental Assistant route with two subjects including English and an accepted science. Contractual obligations differ. Source: [Ministry of Health training opportunities](https://health.gov.gy/training-opportunities/).  
**Professional gate:** the Guyana Dental Council registers dental practitioners, dentist extenders and dental technicians. Source: [Guyana Dental Council](https://health.gov.gy/guyana-dental-council/).  
**First jobs:** supervised dental-assisting, community-dental and technical roles; the actual title must match the completed credential and council category.

## Pharmacist / Pharmacy Technician ✅

UG offers the BSc Pharmacy and a Pharmacy Technician Diploma. The degree page publishes specific CSEC/CAPE science requirements; after training, professional registration and any required supervised practice apply. Sources: [UG BSc Pharmacy](https://cms.uog.edu.gy/department-pharmacy/bachelor-science-pharmacy), [UG offered programmes](https://www.turkeyenonline.uog.edu.gy/srms/student/prospective/about_applying/offeredprogs.php).

## Optometrist ✅

UG's BSc Optometry has multiple routes, including school-leaver and relevant technician/associate pathways. Use its detailed live criteria because the accepted subject combinations, experience and mature-entry rules differ by route. Source: [UG BSc Optometry](https://cms.uog.edu.gy/school-medicine/bachelor-science-optometry). The Ministry of Health states that the Medical Council of Guyana currently handles new optometrist registration and annual licence renewal, and the Council publishes a register of optometrists and opticians. Sources: [Ministry of Health optometry notice](https://health.gov.gy/press_release/health-minister-gao-discusses-independent-registration-of-optometrists-in-guyana/), [Medical Council registers](https://medicalcouncil.org.gy/index.php/registers). The unsupported “one optometrist per 75,000” statistic has been removed.

## Psychologist / Clinical Psychologist ✅

UG offers Psychology training, but clinical practice normally requires training beyond an undergraduate degree. Contrary to the earlier draft, Psychologist and Clinical Psychologist are listed professions under the **Allied Health Professions Council**. Sources: [Allied Health Professions Council](https://health.gov.gy/allied-health-professions-council/), [AHPC registration](https://www.alliedhealthguyana.com/register).

## Medical Laboratory and Environmental Health ✅

Relevant UG programmes and Ministry clinical/technical routes exist. These occupations are not unregulated degree-only careers: the Allied Health Professions Council covers Medical Laboratory Technician/Technologist and Environmental Health Assistant/Officer categories. Always pair the education route with the exact current council registration category. Sources: [Ministry of Health training opportunities](https://health.gov.gy/training-opportunities/), [AHPC professions](https://www.alliedhealthguyana.com/about).

## Respiratory Therapist ✅

UG currently lists a BSc Respiratory Therapy, introduced from 2024–2025. This pathway was missing from the earlier guide. Check the current admission page and allied-health registration requirements. Source: [UG Respiratory Therapy](https://cms.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/257/programmes/1157/details).

## Nutritionist / Dietitian / Food Scientist ✅

Carnegie offers nutrition-related training, while UG offers BSc Food Science. Nutritionist and Dietitian are Allied Health Professions Council categories, so training alone is not the complete professional pathway. Sources: [UG Food Science](https://fns.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/5/programmes/895/details), [Allied Health Professions Council](https://health.gov.gy/allied-health-professions-council/).

## Veterinarian 🟡

No current local veterinary degree was confirmed. Treat veterinary medicine as a regional/international study pathway unless a current Guyanese degree or formal scholarship arrangement is identified. Guyana School of Agriculture certificates and diplomas in animal or livestock production support agricultural work but do not qualify someone as a veterinarian.

## Social Worker ✅

UG's Social Work degree and associate-level routes are relevant to government, hospital, school, community and NGO work. The vacancy controls the exact qualification and experience requirements; do not imply that every social-work role has the same professional gate.

# CATEGORY D: ENGINEERING, TECHNOLOGY & THE BUILT ENVIRONMENT

## Civil, Mechanical and Electrical Engineer ✅

UG now publishes programme-specific direct-entry bachelor's routes. Civil Engineering requires five CSEC subjects including English, Mathematics and Physics; Mechanical publishes English, Mathematics and Physics or Chemistry among its core routes; Electrical has its own subject combinations. Generic older UG pages still describe diploma-first structures, so the **specific current programme page** controls. Sources: [BSc Civil Engineering](https://uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/71/programmes/1180/details), [BSc Mechanical Engineering](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/73/programmes/1151/details), [BSc Electrical Engineering](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/72/programmes/755/details).

Guyana does not currently have the standard mandatory engineering-registration board used in many other countries, and GAPE membership is voluntary. However, the earlier statement that engineers do not appear on GRA's practice-certificate list was false: engineers practising privately for reward are included in GRA's **Tax Practice Certificate** regime. That tax certificate is not a professional engineering licence. Source: [GRA Tax Practice Certificate](https://gra.gov.gy/professionals-tax-practice-certificate/).

## Petroleum Engineer / Petroleum Technician ✅

UG currently separates an Associate of Science in Petroleum Engineering from the Bachelor of Applied Science in Petroleum Engineering; the bachelor's principal route builds on the relevant associate qualification. Do not combine it with Geology as one bachelor's degree. Sources: [UG Associate Petroleum Engineering](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/254/programmes/898/details), [UG Bachelor of Applied Science Petroleum Engineering](https://fot.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/254/programmes/1000/details).  
**Employers:** current operators, contractors and supply-chain firms. Replace “Hess” with **Chevron**, which completed its Hess acquisition in July 2025. Source: [Chevron acquisition](https://www.chevron.com/newsroom/2025/q3/chevron-completes-acquisition-of-hess-corporation).

## Geologist / Mining Professional ✅

UG currently offers BSc Applied and Exploration Geology. Technical and safety training may also be available through mining-sector institutions, but an unsourced claim that “over 90% of trainees secure jobs” has been removed. Source: [UG Applied and Exploration Geology](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/254/programmes/1165/details). First jobs include field/laboratory assistant, junior geologist, mine technician and regulatory roles, subject to vacancy requirements.

## Architect ✅

The earlier direct-entry description was wrong. UG's BSc Architecture ordinarily requires a Diploma or Associate qualification in Architectural and Building Technology, including the published design/GPA requirements, or an accepted technical qualification and portfolio route. The Associate programme is the principal CSEC-entry pathway. Sources: [UG BSc Architecture](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/70/programmes/905/details), [UG Associate Architectural and Building Technology](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/70/programmes/884/details).

## Land Surveyor 🟡

**Forms 1–3:** strengthen Mathematics, Geography, science, spatial reasoning and outdoor fieldwork habits.  
**Forms 4–5:** Mathematics, English, Geography, Physics, Technical Drawing and IT are useful.  
**Post-CSEC:** surveying and land-administration programmes change across UG and technical providers. Confirm the active programme and any statutory registration or supervised-practice requirement before publishing a fixed pathway.

## Quantity Surveyor / Construction Manager 🟡

**Forms 1–3:** combine numeracy, communication, technical drawing and organised project work.  
**Forms 4–5:** Mathematics, English, Technical Drawing, Physics, Principles of Accounts and Principles of Business are useful.  
**Post-CSEC:** architectural/building technology, civil engineering, construction, costing or project-management routes may lead into the field. UG's Architecture Department explicitly teaches construction measurement and costing, construction/project management, building services and BIM, but this is not evidence of a stand-alone quantity-surveying degree. Source: [UG Department of Architecture](https://fot.uog.edu.gy/department-architecture-0).  
**First jobs:** estimating assistant, site/project assistant, measurement technician and junior construction coordinator; senior titles require experience.

## Draughtsperson / CAD or BIM Technician ✅

**Forms 1–3:** practise Mathematics, visualisation, drawing and computer skills.  
**Forms 4–5:** Technical Drawing, Mathematics, English, IT, Physics and Visual Arts are useful. Build a small portfolio of plans and 3D models.  
**Post-CSEC:** UG's Associate of Science in Architectural and Building Technology is a relevant route, alongside current technical-institute drafting/CAD programmes. The UG department identifies CAD and Building Information Modelling as core areas. Sources: [UG Associate Architectural and Building Technology](https://registry.uog.edu.gy/srms/departments/70/programmes/884/details), [UG Department of Architecture](https://fot.uog.edu.gy/department-architecture-0).  
**First jobs:** CAD technician, architectural technician, drawing-office assistant and BIM trainee.

## Occupational Safety and Health Officer ✅

**Forms 1–3:** develop science awareness, communication, observation, responsibility and first-aid knowledge.  
**Forms 4–5:** English, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics and technical subjects are useful.  
**Post-CSEC:** UG currently lists a Diploma and bachelor-completion routes in Occupational Health and Safety; approved industry certifications and supervised site experience may also be important. Source: [UG offered programmes](https://www.turkeyenonline.uog.edu.gy/srms/student/prospective/about_applying/offeredprogs.php). The Ministry of Labour's OSH service explains the national workplace-safety framework. Source: [Ministry of Labour OSH](https://labour.gov.gy/osh/).  
**First jobs:** safety assistant, site-safety representative and HSE trainee; “HSE manager” is an experienced role.

## Software / Web / Mobile Developer ✅

**Forms 1–3:** build Mathematics, English, typing, logic and simple coding projects.  
**Forms 4–5:** Mathematics and IT/Computer Science are especially useful; maintain a portfolio on a code-hosting or project platform.

Do not frame a degree as a requirement unless a particular appointed public office legally requires it.

## Religious Vocation ⚠️

Training and recognition are denomination-specific. Routes may involve seminaries, religious institutes, apprenticeship/formation and community approval. The system should ask for the faith tradition before recommending a pathway.

## Migration and International Recognition 🟡

For nursing, medicine, teaching, engineering, accounting, aviation and other regulated careers, a Guyanese qualification does not automatically authorise overseas practice. Store destination-country accreditation, examination, supervised-experience and immigration requirements separately from the Guyana route.

# RULES FOR THE SITE'S GAP-ANALYSIS ENGINE

1. **Use programme-specific sources.** A current programme page outranks an older faculty overview or generic admissions page.
2. **Use an intake year.** Admissions, scholarships, fees and recruitment rules must expire or enter a re-verification queue.
3. **Separate recommendations from requirements.** “Science stream” is advice; the actual subject/grade rule comes from the institution.
4. **Do not hard-code approximate ages.** Calculate a range from entry age, programme duration, internship and likely delay, and label it as an estimate.
5. **Do not treat an employer list as a hiring pathway.** Store vacancy source, minimum experience, location and closing date separately.
6. **Model professional regulation.** Education, internship, examination, registration and licence renewal are separate nodes.
7. **Model region and delivery.** Store campus, region, online/in-person status, relocation, accommodation and transport support.
8. **Model costs honestly.** Separate tuition, application, examination, equipment, travel, accommodation and professional-registration costs.
9. **Keep portfolio/talent pathways parallel to school.** Sports and creative careers should not make students abandon general education prematurely.
10. **Distinguish credentials.** CSEC, CAPE, GTEE, CVQ, NVQ, certificates of competency, diplomas, degrees and professional licences are not interchangeable.
