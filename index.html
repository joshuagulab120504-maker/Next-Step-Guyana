About us

No student in Guyana should have to figure out their future alone.

Preparing for CSEC and CAPE is hard enough. Knowing what to do after leaves many students with big questions and very little guidance. What should I study? Where can I apply? How do I even begin?

Next Step Guyana was founded by Joshua Gulab and Aniyah Couchman, Guyanese students and CAPE Top Performers who remember that feeling: the uncertainty, the pressure, and the quiet hope that someone would help make things clearer.

Today we are growing Guyana's first comprehensive network of mentors who have walked your road before. Free mentorship, live sessions and honest advice, so you can take your next step with confidence. This is not just a platform. It is a community built on care, understanding and the belief that your future matters.
