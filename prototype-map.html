Core journey demonstrated

  1. Complete a 60-second self-check without an account.
  2. Receive an explorer identity and several explainable Northern Star options.
  3. Choose a current Star and build a mini-LinkedIn profile of subjects, grades and experiences.
  4. Navi identifies gaps, proposes milestones and surfaces relevant mentor decisions.
  5. Get inspired by matched mentors, join pods and unlock focused support through preparation and progress.
  6. Mentors see new inspirations and aggregate outcomes, then contribute advice to the shared feed.
  7. Admins verify mentors, stories and content while managing permissions and safeguarding.

Prototype boundaries

  • All names, statistics, availability and recommendations are illustrative.
  • No authentication, database, WhatsApp, calendar, video or AI service is connected.
  • Production recommendations require verified Guyana-specific data and professional review.
  • Safeguarding, consent, privacy, accessibility and permissions require formal implementation specifications.
  • Every page is responsive and designed for keyboard, touch and mobile navigation review.
